## Worst level

`traffic_m3_l10_rush_hour`: depth 4, 13 visited / 9 expanded, ~85 ms. It is the
largest board (7×4, 4 entities, 4 items) and the deepest solution. Search size
is still tiny relative to the 200000-state bound.

## Assessment

- **Bounded:** every level completes in well under one second; the largest
  search explores 13 states against a 200000-state cap.
- **Deterministic:** repeated solves of all ten levels produce identical status,
  depth, visited and expanded counts, and identical solution command lists.
- **Practical:** total solve time for the whole official pack is ~0.26 s.
- **No search explosion.** No level shows a pathological frontier.

Optimization beyond straightforward BFS is therefore **not** justified for the
current content (per the M5 rule: optimize only with measured evidence). Larger
generated levels are the new M6 generation engine's concern.
